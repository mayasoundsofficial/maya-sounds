Maya Sounds Uploader

A private tool for the YouTube channel "Maya Sounds"

Maya Sounds Uploader is a small personal tool used only by the owner of the "Maya Sounds" YouTube channel. It uploads the channel owner's own music videos to their own channel and sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist, audience setting and publish schedule.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. It uses the official YouTube Data API with the channel owner's permission.
